# One Citizen One Question projection

Participants may submit plain-language questions tied to an investigation. A moderator must approve a question before it appears publicly. A reviewed answer requires at least one HTTPS source. Until then its status is **unanswered**, with no implication of agency wrongdoing. Obvious requests for personal contact or identifying details are excluded; a real moderation queue must assess context and abuse more broadly.

Authenticated submission, rate limits, abuse review, source retention and the independent One Question integration contract remain future work. This module is a public projection only.
