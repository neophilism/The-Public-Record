# Silence Clock public exhibit

The exhibit separates calendar days since a participant's request, calendar days since a verified agency receipt, and an externally reviewed estimate of statutory working days. It stops elapsed counters at a recorded terminal response, marks evidence feeds stale after seven days, and shows reported tolling or extension context. It never concludes that an agency violated the law.

The working-day estimator and legal evidence live in the separate TPR-24 work. Actual receipt provenance, holiday rules, agency-specific regulations and verified tolling notices must be integrated before a public estimate can be shown as reviewed. A missing receipt date leaves the receipt and working-day measures unknown. See [5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(6)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section552&num=0&edition=prelim).
