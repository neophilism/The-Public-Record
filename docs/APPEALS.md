# Appeal assistance boundary

An appeal starts from a retained adverse determination. Later appeal events append to its timeline and never erase that determination. The helper produces text for the participant to review and file themselves; it sends nothing to an agency.

Agency filing instructions appear only when an operator-reviewed official HTTPS `.gov` URL belongs to the same agency component. Otherwise the participant must verify current instructions in the agency letter or official site. The interface offers [OGIS](https://www.archives.gov/ogis) as a possible mediation resource. No date is presented as a certain deadline from this module. Federal FOIA's general appeal window must be at least 90 days after an adverse determination, but agency rules and evidence of receipt require separate review: [5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(6)(A)(i)(III)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section552&num=0&edition=prelim).

Authenticated private storage, agency-specific reviewed instructions, source evidence and end-to-end user workflow remain to be integrated.
